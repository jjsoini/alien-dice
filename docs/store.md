# Dice

Beautiful 3D dice extension for Alien RPG

- Roll base and stress dice. Click a die to add it to the pool, or roll it straight into the dice already in the tray.
- Push a roll: a stress die is added and every die that isn't a 6 is rerolled. You can push again, but not after a facehugger.
- See your successes and PANIC at a glance.
- Draw initiative cards from a shared 10 card deck. Players draw one card, the GM can draw several for NPCs and shuffles the deck.
- Roll hidden, see the other players' trays, and use it on your phone.
